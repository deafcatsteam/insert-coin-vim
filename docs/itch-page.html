What it is

INSERT COIN is a Vim trainer dressed as a 1987 operator terminal. It runs in your browser. You do not need to know Vim to start.

How it works

  • Each level shows a text and a target. The differences are highlighted.
  • Change the text into the target with real Vim keys.
  • The level ends as soon as the text matches. Any solution counts.
  • ★ for finishing, ★★ for reaching par, ★★★ for matching the shortest known solution.
  • Keys are counted like on VimGolf: Esc and Enter count as one key each.

Also on your terminal

  • A real Vim engine (CodeMirror 6 with its Vim mode), not a simulation of a few keys.
  • Two hints per level, a Show solution button and a Skip level button. Nobody stays stuck.
  • A replay of the reference solution, key by key, after each level.
  • A daily challenge: the same level for every operator on a given day (UTC).
  • A review mode: levels you skipped or barely passed come back later.
  • A CRT look you can tune: scanlines, glow, flicker and screen curve.
  • Arcade beeps, off by default.
  • In 9 languages: English, French, Spanish, German, Portuguese (Brazil), Italian, Russian, Simplified Chinese and Japanese.

You need

  • A desktop browser.
  • A physical keyboard. Any layout works (AZERTY, QWERTZ, QWERTY…).

Privacy

No account, no ads, no tracking. Your progress stays in your browser. You can export it to a file and import it again.
